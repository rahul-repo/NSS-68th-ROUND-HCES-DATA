\section{\textcolor{blue}{Objectives of the Study}}

The present study uses data from the \textbf{\textcolor{deepgreen}{68th Round Household Consumer Expenditure Survey (HCES)}} to examine differences in consumption behaviour and socioeconomic patterns between \textbf{\textcolor{deepgreen}{West Bengal}} and \textbf{\textcolor{deepgreen}{Assam}}. The objectives are:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item To test whether cereal consumption differs between the two states and consider whether differences in local cultivation may influence expenditure.
    \item To assess variation in non-vegetarian consumption prevalence and discuss whether cultural or religious composition contributes to the observed pattern.
    \item To compare Monthly Per Capita Expenditure (MPCE) across the two states as an indicator of relative economic well-being.
    \item To examine state-wise addiction behaviour and analyse its association with social group categories.
\end{enumerate}
